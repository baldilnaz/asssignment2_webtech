Event Menu Information

Event menus are arranged individually. The final price depends on the number of guests, the number of tables and the dishes selected for the event.

The selection of salads and some other dishes may vary depending on the event and current availability.

Please Note

Alcohol and fruit are not included in the standard event menu and may be provided separately by the event hosts.

Wedding and other celebration cakes are also arranged separately by the hosts and are not included in the standard menu.
