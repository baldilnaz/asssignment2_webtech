Celebrate at Arai

A spacious indoor venue for gatherings and special occasions.

Arai Restaurant has a spacious indoor hall that can be used for different celebrations and gatherings. The room includes guest tables and a large central area. Decorative chandeliers, ceiling details and lighting help create an atmosphere suitable for special occasions.

Every event can have different requirements, including the number of guests, date and table arrangement. Visitors should contact the restaurant directly to discuss current availability and details.
