About Arai

A restaurant and event venue in Satpayev.

Arai Restaurant opened in 2021 and welcomes guests for dining, family gatherings and special events.

The restaurant offers two event halls, a main hall for larger gatherings and a smaller hall for more private events.

Guest parking is available for up to 30 cars.
