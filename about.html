About Arai

Restaurant and event venue in Satpayev

Arai Restaurant

Since 2021

Arai Restaurant opened in Satpayev in 2021.

The restaurant combines everyday dining with spaces for private gatherings, family occasions and larger events.

Arai has two separate event halls, allowing guests to choose a space according to the size and type of their gathering.

Two Spaces for Different Occasions

Arai offers a main hall and a smaller hall for different types of gatherings.

Main Hall

The main hall is the larger event space at Arai and is designed for larger gatherings and celebrations.

Capacity: 350 guests

Small Hall

The small hall provides a more compact setting for smaller gatherings and private occasions.

Capacity: 170 guests
